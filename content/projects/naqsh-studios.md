## Role

As a **Video Editor** with **NAQSH Studios**, the film-making club at Gautam Buddha University, I took rushes from shoot day to a finished short film.

## Editing for story

- **Assembly → rough cut → fine cut.** First get every story beat on the timeline, then cut for rhythm.
- **Pacing.** Let emotional beats breathe; tighten transitions between them. J- and L-cuts carry dialogue across cuts so scenes flow.
- **Coverage choices.** Picking the take that serves the scene, not the one with the cleanest focus.

## Post-production workflow

- Proxy media for smooth editing on a laptop, relinked to originals for the grade.
- Shot-matching across cameras before building a look.
- Dialogue clean-up and room-tone fills on the **Fairlight** page.
- Final deliverables exported for festival screening and social cut-downs.
